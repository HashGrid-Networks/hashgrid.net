How Does Bitcoin Mining Work?

Bitcoin mining is the process by which new Bitcoins are created and transactions are verified on the blockchain network. Miners use specialized hardware to solve complex cryptographic puzzles, competing to add new blocks to the Bitcoin blockchain. When a miner successfully solves a puzzle, they earn Bitcoin rewards and transaction fees.

Bitcoin Mining Explained: Key Concepts

Hash Rate: The computational power used in Bitcoin mining, measured in hashes per second (H/s). Higher hash rates increase mining success probability.

Mining Difficulty: A measure of how difficult it is to find a hash below the target. Bitcoin adjusts difficulty every 2016 blocks to maintain consistent block times.

Block Rewards: The Bitcoin amount miners receive for successfully mining a block. Currently set at 3.125 BTC per block (as of 2024 halving).

Mining Pools: Groups of miners who combine computational resources to increase chances of earning rewards, then share profits proportionally.
