## Key Topics We Cover

### What is Bitcoin Mining?
Bitcoin mining is the process by which new Bitcoins are created and transactions are verified on the blockchain. Miners use specialized hardware to solve cryptographic puzzles, earning Bitcoin rewards.

### How Does Bitcoin Mining Work?
Miners compete to solve complex mathematical problems using ASIC hardware. The first to solve it adds a new block to the blockchain and receives Bitcoin rewards plus transaction fees.
